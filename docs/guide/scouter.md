# Scouters

## Every visit

### Check gear out

Tap **Scan** and point the camera at a code. The card shows the item; tap **Check out**. The first check-out asks for
the event: tap one from the list, type a name and tap **Create**, or tap **No event**. Later check-outs use the same
event until four hours pass with no move. To switch events, tap **Change** at the top. The confirmation names the event.
Checking out from an item's page asks the same way. **Events** shows the event check-outs go to, with **Change** beside
it.

### Return gear

Tap **Scan** and point at each code. Gear you have shows where it goes; tap **Return** and put it there. Gear someone
else has offers **Return** and **Take over**: pick the one you mean. Any order, over as many visits as you like.

### Gear you weigh, like a propane tank

Some gear is set up to be weighed, so the app can say how much is left. When you tap **Return**, type what it weighs now
and pick **lb** or **kg**. If you weighed or refilled it since it went out, Return skips the question. No scale? Tap
**Unknown**; the last weight stays as it was. A scan shows **Weigh** and **Refilled** too. On its page, **Weigh**
records a weight any time (or **Unknown**), and **Refilled** says it is full again. The page and the list say "About 8
lb left (40%)".

### Take some of a stack

Some gear is counted, not labelled one by one: bowls, mugs, tent pegs. Scan the stack's code if it has one, or find it
by search, and tap **Check out**. It asks how many — starting at 1 — and the event, same as any check-out. To take them
all, like a whole bag of PFDs, tap **All**. **Return** offers the count you have out; confirm it, or change it if you
are keeping some back.

The app will not check out more than it shows in. If there are more, tap **Recount** and count them first.

### No sticker on it?

Search for it first. It may already be in the app, or have lost its sticker.

- **Found it.** Stick a fresh code on it, scan it, tap **Put it on an existing item**, and pick it.
- **Not there.** Stick a code on it, scan it, and tap **Create a new item**. Type its name, pick a home, and tap
  **Save**. A name is enough if you are in a hurry.
- **One of several,** like tents. Tick **We have several of these** before you save; this one becomes #1. For the next,
  scan its code, tap **Add a unit of…**, and tap the name. Type the number first if one is already written on the gear.
- **Counted gear,** like mugs or tent pegs. Do not sticker each one. Tap **New item** on the home screen, tick **We have
  several of these**, pick **A stack we count**, and say how many. Then put one code on the bin: scan it, tap **Put it
  on an existing item**, and pick the stack.
- **Cannot take a sticker,** too small or always wet. Tap **New item** on the home screen and save it with no code. Find
  it by search to check it out; in a packing session, tap its row in the list.

Then check it out as usual.

### Find gear

Type a name in the **Search** box at the top of the home screen. To browse the whole list, open the menu and tap **All
items**, then use **Filters** to narrow by location, shelf, category, or in and out.

## When something is off

### Report damage

Tap **Report a problem** on the item's card and say what is wrong. Then tap **Return**, or **Check out** if it is in the
locker; the ticket is raised with that move. To raise it without moving the item, tap **Save without moving**. The item
is flagged for whoever takes it next.

Raised one by mistake? Open the ticket and tap **Delete ticket…** twice. This works until someone else comments on it,
adds a photo or moves it; after that, ask an Admin.

### A sticker fell off

Open the item, tap **Replace QR code**, and scan a fresh sticker. Its history, tickets and events stay with it. The old
sticker still opens the same item if it turns up.

### Gear that has gone missing

Open the item and tap **Mark missing**. It drops off **What is out** until someone scans it or returns it.

### Take gear someone else has

Scan it. The card names who has it; tap **Take over**. It never comes back to the locker, and the history keeps both of
you.

### Send gear home with someone

Once gear is out, for example at camp, scan it and tap **Send home with…**. Use it when someone takes gear home to clean
it, or borrows it. Type the adult who has it, never a Scout's name; the name is required, and names used lately appear
as you type. You stay the holder, so it stays on your list until it is back. For a stack, give the count; send each
person's share on its own. When they bring it back, tap **Back from** and their name; it stays with you and leaves their
home. For a stack, pick the person and the count, then tap **Back from home**. When it goes back to the locker, tap
**Return**. To fix a typo, open the item and tap **Change name**.

### Hand gear to someone else

Scan it and tap **Transfer to…**, then type part of their name and tap it. Works with no signal.

### No signal

Everything except signing in works with no signal. An amber strip counts records still on your device; it clears itself
when you next open the app in signal. Tap it to see what is waiting and why. Records waiting three days stop the app and
ask you to sync.

## Camps

### Plan an event

Open **Events** and tap **New event**. Name it, set the dates, and add gear by searching, or by picking a quantity of
something the group has several of. **Duplicate** on last year's camp saves building the list again.

### Pack for an event

Open **Events**, pick the camp, and tap **Check out**. If it is under way or starts within a week, it is also sitting on
the home screen: tap it there instead. Scan each item; every check-out is under the camp's event, with no question. The
list under the viewfinder shows what is still to pack; tick **Show packed** to check what already went. Tap **Finish**,
which names anything you did not scan. The camp's own page also marks each line packed or not; tick **Not packed** to
see only what is left. From the first day, **Returning** switches the page to what is still out, and it turns on by
itself once the event ends.

Gear checked out under a planned event's name joins its list by itself, from a week before it starts. Took gear under an
event nobody planned? **Events** lists it too. Tap **Add**, and it joins the list, holding that gear.

## Your account

### Sign in

Open the invite link an Admin sends you and choose a password. After that, your email and password. Signing in needs a
connection; using the app does not. Five wrong passwords lock your email for 15 minutes; the app says how long is left,
and an Admin can unlock it sooner.

### Install it

On an iPhone, install the app from the **App Store** link on the home screen. On an iPad, or if there is no link, do
this before you record anything: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Safari loses a tab's unsent records after
seven days without a visit; an installed app does not. Android keeps them either way, so installing there is only a
shortcut: open the browser menu and tap **Add to Home screen** if you want one.

### A lost device

Sign in on the new device and the whole inventory is there. Revoke the old one under **Settings** > **Your devices**.
Anything the old device never sent is gone.

### Email alerts

Open **Settings** > **Notifications** and tick what you want to hear about: gear reported found, a new repair ticket,
someone joining. Each box saves itself. Nothing arrives if the group has not set up mail.

### Connect an AI assistant

Open **Settings** > **AI assistant** and tap **Connect an assistant**; the screen walks you through the rest. It can do
anything your role allows in the app except delete your account, and it needs a connection.

### Delete your account

Open **Settings** > **Delete my account**. You are signed out and cannot sign in again. Other devices sign out the next
time they connect, after sending anything they had not sent. Your name stays on what you did; an Admin can restore your
access. It needs a connection.
