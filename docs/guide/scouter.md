# Scouter

## Every visit

### Check gear out

Tap Scan and point the camera at a code. The card shows the item; tap Check out. The first check-out asks for the event:
tap one from the list, type a name and tap Create, or tap No event. Later check-outs use the same event until four hours
pass with no move. To switch events, tap Change at the top. Checking out from an item's page asks the same way.

### Return gear

Tap Scan and point at each code. Gear you have shows where it goes; tap Return and put it there. Gear someone else has
offers Return and Take over: pick the one you mean. Any order, over as many visits as you like.

### Take some of a stack

Some gear is counted, not labelled one by one: bowls, mugs, tent pegs. Scan the stack's code if it has one, or find it
by search, and tap Check out. It asks how many — starting at 1 — and the event, same as any check-out. Return offers the
count you have out; confirm it, or change it if you are keeping some back.

### No sticker on it?

Search for it first. It may already be in the app, or have lost its sticker.

- **Found it.** Stick a fresh code on it, scan it, tap "Put it on an existing item", and pick it.
- **Not there.** Stick a code on it, scan it, and tap Create a new item. Type its name, pick a home, and Save. A name is
  enough if you are in a hurry.
- **One of several,** like tents. Tick "We have several of these" before you save; this one becomes #1. For the next,
  scan its code, tap "Another of…", and tap the name. Type the number first if one is already written on the gear.
- **Counted gear,** like mugs or tent pegs. Do not sticker each one. Tap New item on the home screen, tick "We have
  several of these", pick A stack we count, and say how many. Then put one code on the bin: scan it, tap "Put it on an
  existing item", and pick the stack.
- **Cannot take a sticker,** too small or always wet. Tap New item on the home screen and save it with no code. Find it
  by search to check it out; in a packing session, tap its row in the list.

Then check it out as usual.

### Find gear

Type a name in the Search box at the top of the home screen. To browse the whole list, open the menu and tap All items,
then use Filters to narrow by location, shelf, category, or in and out.

## When something is off

### Report damage

Tap Report a problem on the item's card and say what is wrong. Then tap Return, or Check out if it is in the locker; the
ticket is raised with that move. To raise it without moving the item, tap Save without moving. The item is flagged for
whoever takes it next.

### A sticker fell off

Open the item, tap Replace QR code, and scan a fresh sticker. Its history, tickets and reservations stay with it. The
old sticker still opens the same item if it turns up.

### Gear that has gone missing

Open the item and tap Mark missing. It drops off What is out until someone scans it or returns it.

### Take gear someone else has

Scan it. The card names who has it; tap Take over. It never comes back to the locker, and the history keeps both of you.

### Hand gear to someone else

Scan it and tap Transfer to…, then type part of their name and tap it. Works with no signal.

### No signal

Everything except signing in works with no signal. An amber strip counts records still on your device; it clears itself
when you next open the app in signal. Records waiting three days stop the app and ask you to sync.

## Camps

### Plan a reservation

Open Reservations and tap New reservation. Name the event, set the dates, and add gear by searching, or by picking a
quantity of something the group has several of. Duplicate on last year's camp saves building the list again.

### Pack for a reservation

Open Reservations, pick the camp, and tap Check out. If it is under way or starts within a week, it is also sitting on
the home screen: tap it there instead. Scan each item; every check-out is under the camp's event, with no question. The
list under the viewfinder shows what is still to pack, with a "Show packed" toggle if you want to check what already
went. Tap Finish, which names anything you did not scan. The camp's own page also marks each line packed or not; tick
Not packed to see only what is left.

Took gear under an event nobody reserved? Reservations lists it under Out with no reservation. Tap Reserve, and the
event gets a reservation holding that gear.

## Your account

### Sign in

Open the invite link an Admin sends you and choose a password. After that, your email and password. Signing in needs a
connection; using the app does not. Five wrong passwords lock your email for 15 minutes; the app says how long is left,
and an Admin can unlock it sooner.

### Install it

On an iPhone, install the app from the App Store link on the home screen. On an iPad, or if there is no link, do this
before you record anything: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Safari loses a tab's unsent records after seven days
without a visit; an installed app does not. Android keeps them either way, so installing there is only a shortcut: open
the browser menu and tap Add to Home screen if you want one.

### A lost device

Sign in on the new device and the whole inventory is there. Revoke the old one under Settings, Your devices. Anything
the old device never sent is gone.

### Email alerts

Open Notifications in Settings and tick what you want to hear about: gear reported found, a new repair ticket, someone
joining. Each box saves itself. Nothing arrives if the group has not set up mail.

### Delete your account

Settings, Delete my account. You are signed out and cannot sign in again. Other devices sign out the next time they
connect, after sending anything they had not sent. Your name stays on what you did; an Admin can restore your access. It
needs a connection.
