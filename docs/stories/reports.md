# Reports

Answering the question the group cannot answer today.

## S-RPT-01 What is out, and who has it

The Quartermaster opens one report and sees every item currently out and who took it. This is the reason the project
exists.

- One report, no filters required to make it useful
- Names the holder and the event
- Sortable by holder, item, time out, or reservation

Covers: FR-RPT-01, FR-RPT-12

## S-RPT-02 What has been gone too long

Gear out longer than the group's set period is flagged, with no per-item dates to maintain.

- One group-wide period, e.g. 30 days
- Overdue items are listed

Covers: FR-OUT-14, FR-RPT-05

## S-RPT-03 Find things before camp

A Scouter filters the inventory to tents that are in, narrowed to the Warm locker.

- Filter by category, location, shelf, and status

Covers: FR-INV-08

## S-RPT-04 A stock check

Walking the lockers with the app, the Quartermaster finds items recorded as in but not where they should be.

- Items in but not at their home are listed

Covers: FR-RPT-09
