# Public

Someone outside the group finds our gear. They have no account and never will.

## S-PUB-01 A stranger finds a tent

Someone finds a tent at a campsite and scans the sticker with their phone camera.

- They see the item name, the group name, and how to make contact
- They see no member names, no prices, and no history
- They are not asked to sign in

Covers: FR-PUB-01, NFR-SEC-03

## S-PUB-02 They report it

The finder leaves a note saying where it is and how to reach them.

- A note and an optional contact detail are enough
- The form resists spam and abuse

Covers: FR-PUB-02, FR-PUB-04

## S-PUB-03 We hear about it

The report reaches the Quartermaster inside the app as something to act on, not as an email nobody reads.

- Found reports appear in the app
- They are actionable, not just a notification

Covers: FR-PUB-03

## S-PUB-04 What the public cannot do

A finder cannot check gear in or out, and cannot raise a repair ticket.

- The public surface is one form: report found gear
- Everything else needs an account

Covers: FR-PUB-05, FR-PUB-06
