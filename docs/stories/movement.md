# Movement

Check-out and check-in. This is the loop that has to be faster than walking off with a tent.

## S-MOVE-01 Take gear to camp

A Scouter taps Scan and scans six items. Each scan shows the item; one tap checks it out. After the first check-out, the
top of the screen asks which event, offering tonight's calendar entry; one tap picks it.

- The event is asked once and applies to everything scanned before and after the answer
- Skipping the event is one tap, and ignoring it loses only the event
- Each scan shows the item so it can be checked against what is in hand
- The confirmation names the event, and **Events** shows and changes it
- One tap confirms and the scanner is ready again
- No signal is needed

Covers: FR-OUT-01, FR-OUT-03, FR-OUT-04, FR-OUT-05, FR-OUT-06, FR-OUT-29, FR-OUT-30, FR-OUT-32, FR-OUT-33, FR-OUT-37,
FR-OUT-38, FR-OUT-43, FR-OUT-44, FR-OFF-01

## S-MOVE-02 Return gear

A Scouter unpacking the trailer taps Scan and scans each item. Gear they took shows its home and one tap returns it.
Gear someone else took offers Return and Take over side by side, with no default.

- Gear the Scouter holds is returned in one tap
- The card shows the item's home so it can be put away
- Gear held by someone else is never moved by a guess
- Returns never ask for an event
- Items go back in any order, over any number of sessions

Covers: FR-OUT-06, FR-OUT-08, FR-OUT-10, FR-OUT-31, FR-OUT-34, FR-OUT-35, FR-OUT-39

## S-MOVE-03 Return someone else's gear

Whoever drove the trailer returns gear that three different people checked out.

- Anyone can return anything
- The record shows who took it and who returned it

Covers: FR-OUT-08, FR-USR-05

## S-MOVE-04 Something came back broken

While checking in a tent, a Scouter notices a broken zip. They raise a repair ticket without leaving the flow.

- A ticket can be raised from the check-in screen
- The item is then flagged for anyone who tries to take it
- Scanning continues afterwards

Covers: FR-OUT-09, FR-REP-01, FR-REP-05

## S-MOVE-05 Fix a detail on the way past

A Scouter scans a stove on the shelf and notices it is stored in the wrong place. They correct its home from the card,
and raise a repair ticket on a dented pot, without moving either.

- Any field can be edited from the scan card
- A repair ticket needs no move
- The change is confirmed, not silent
- The edit is recorded against the item

Covers: FR-OUT-11, FR-OUT-27, FR-USR-05

## S-MOVE-06 Gear with no sticker

A Scouter takes a tarp that was never labelled. They find it by search and check it out.

- Items can be checked in and out without scanning
- Search finds them as the user types

Covers: FR-OUT-02, FR-OUT-07, FR-INV-07

## S-MOVE-07 Someone already has it

A Scouter scans a stove that is already out. The card names who has it and since when, and offers Take over.

- The current holder is shown
- The gear can be taken over rather than double-booked

Covers: FR-OUT-12, FR-OUT-35, FR-OUT-39

## S-MOVE-08 Lent to a parent

A Scouter checks out a tent and lends it to a parent for the weekend. They tap **Send home with** and type the parent's
name.

- Sending it home with a name does not change the holder, event or reservation
- The name can be corrected later
- No account is needed for the person holding it

Covers: FR-OUT-46, FR-OUT-50

## S-MOVE-09 The sticker came off

A tarp comes back with its label missing. A Scouter finds the item by search, sticks a fresh code on it, and binds it.

- An unassigned code can be bound to an item that already exists
- The item keeps its identity, its history, and its reservations
- The old code still resolves to the same item if it turns up later

Covers: FR-TAG-04, FR-TAG-05, FR-TAG-07

## S-MOVE-10 It never came back

A stove is recorded as out, but nobody knows who has it. The Quartermaster marks it missing rather than deleting it or
leaving it on the out list forever.

- Missing gear drops off the what-is-out report
- It stays in the inventory and can still be found by search
- Scanning it or returning it clears the mark

Covers: FR-INV-19, FR-RPT-01

## S-MOVE-11 Hand it on

A Scouter leaving camp early has the first-aid kit. They scan it and transfer it to the Scouter staying on.

- Any active member can be picked, by typing part of the name
- Whoever they last handed gear to is at the top
- The list works with no signal
- The history shows who handed it to whom

Covers: FR-OUT-26, FR-OUT-28, FR-OUT-36
